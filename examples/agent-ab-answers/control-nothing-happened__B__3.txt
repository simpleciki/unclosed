WHY THE TWO RUNS DISAGREE
Because no one said when, the tool has to self-select "the worst bucket in view," and per the skill's own design a self-selected window can never be certified real (SUBSTANTIATED) -- only ever refuted (ARTIFACT) or left open (UNDECIDABLE). Here that self-selection isn't even stable: the underlying data is fixed, but "last six hours" is measured against a clock that keeps moving, so which bucket counts as "worst" -- and therefore which of two very different-looking verdicts comes back -- depends on the minute you happen to query, not on anything that changed in the system.
