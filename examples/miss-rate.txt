3. CONCENTRATION DETECTION FLOOR  (known limit A, measured)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The probe confirms a concentration when the observed subgroup gap is larger
  than shuffling the group labels produces, at a declared 5% rate. Each rung
  below is one strength of planted concentration, sized as the target's median
  excess over the rest as a share of the window's own median rise. That share
  is a property of the generated data, not a threshold the probe uses -- the
  constant it used to be is what this replaced. The first rung plants nothing.

  x010                  14.4%          0/5       0%   below the floor: a real concentration, never established
  x012                  23.8%          1/5      20%   partial: detection is not yet reliable at this strength
  x014                  46.8%          4/5      80%   partial: detection is not yet reliable at this strength

  FLOOR: reliable detection begins at a true share of 68.3% of the
  window's own median rise. Below that the probe reports 'elevated, not
  established' and the concentration is missed.

  DIRECTION: NOT one-sided. 7 run(s) confirmed a concentration where none
  was planted. Section 2 names which cases; the risks below explain both
  sources, and they are different -- only one of them is the declared rate.

  WHAT THIS REPLACED. The probe previously required the excess to reach 50% of
  the window's own median rise. That constant failed in both directions at once,
  and this harness measured both: it found a 46.8% concentration in 2 runs of 5,
  and -- because a window whose median moved only by noise still has a median
  rise above zero -- it confirmed a concentration on the negative control in 3
  runs of 5. Against the null, that negative control is now correct in 5 of 5
  and the 46.8% rung is found in 4 of 5.

  RISKS THIS CARRIES. Each is a way the null can be wrong, stated with what the
  corpus measured about it rather than left for a reader to discover.

  1. THE LABELS MUST BE EXCHANGEABLE, AND SOMETIMES THEY ARE NOT. Shuffling
     asks whether the gap is larger than one produced by labels that mean
     nothing. It does not ask whether the gap is NEW. A subgroup that is
     permanently slower carries a large gap in every window, including the
     quiet ones, and a test looking only inside the focus window cannot see
     that. Measured on `real-uniform-rise-on-skewed-index` -- one endpoint
     permanently four times slower, then everything rises by the same amount,
     so the gap is unchanged and explains none of the rise -- the probe
     confirms a concentration in 5 runs of 5. Not occasionally. Every time.
     The answer is a null drawn from the baseline windows rather than from
     shuffled labels in the focus window: that measures the gap normally
     present instead of the gap chance produces. It is not built.

  2. THE 5% IS PER DIMENSION, AND FOUR ARE TESTED. The null covers the choice
     of the most extreme value WITHIN a dimension, because it is a null of the
     maximum. It does not cover the choice among the four dimensions. A run
     therefore carries roughly a 1-(0.95)^4 = 18% chance of confirming
     something somewhere when nothing is concentrated anywhere. That is the
     source of the isolated false confirmation on `real-no-discoverable-cause`,
     and it is the declared rate behaving as declared, not an anomaly.

  3. THE NULL IS ESTIMATED ON WHAT WAS READ. The permutation runs over a capped
     sample of the focus window. Observed statistic and null come from the same
     documents, so the comparison stays valid, but on a window larger than the
     cap it is a test on that sample rather than on the window -- and the
     evidence line says so when it happens. No case in this corpus exceeded the
     cap, so nothing here exercises that path.

  deploy landed, an upstream dependency slowed, the host lost resources -- are
